# 5 AI prompts every Canadian freelancer needs

Free sample from the Canadian Freelancer Cashflow Kit. Edit before you send — AI drafts, you own the client.

1. **Rate check:** Act as a pricing coach. Services: [x]. Target clients: [y]. Monthly costs ~$[z] CAD. Suggest hourly, day, and project rates with a one-line rationale each.

2. **Proposal opener:** Write a 120-word proposal opening for [client] that states their problem, the outcome I’ll deliver, and why I’m a fit — no fluff.

3. **Scope creep:** Client asked for [extra] after we agreed [original scope]. Draft a calm change-order note with impact on price and timeline in CAD.

4. **Invoice email:** Friendly invoice email for #[n], $[amount] CAD, due [date], pay by [e-transfer/details].

5. **Overdue 7 days:** Polite but firm payment reminder; assume goodwill; ask for a paid-by date.
